## 初始车型平衡

固定困难 AI、相同控制规则和车辆物理，以 20 Hz 独立模拟各车在各赛道跑三圈，没有碰撞。取每次最佳有效圈，除以该赛道四车最快圈时，再对三条赛道等权平均。数据见 `validation/balance.json`。

| 车型 | 滨海疾线 | 林间折线 | 红土牧场 | 归一化平均 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 高速型 | 51.40 s | 58.80 s | 54.85 s | 1.0244 |
| 灵巧型 | 55.10 s | 55.95 s | 55.10 s | 1.0330 |
| 土路型 | 54.00 s | 56.05 s | 53.65 s | 1.0175 |
| 均衡型 | 52.60 s | 56.15 s | 53.95 s | 1.0108 |

平均成绩的最大/最小比值差约 **2.19%**，落在约 3% 的初始目标内。高速型赢高速赛道，灵巧型赢多弯赛道，土路型赢土路赛道。12 组试跑均完成三圈，路外帧占比为 0。

这是自动驾驶路线下的初始参数检查，不等同于人类玩家的最终平衡验证。实际游戏以 60 Hz 运行，还需比较不同玩家的失误率、完成率和上手难度；圈时目标不能替代这些指标。

## Continuous raised curbs (2026-09-14)

Both road edges now have an 0.85 m raised red/white curb. Shared segment endpoints close bends and the lap seam; convex collision shapes follow ground, ramps and bridge heights. Curbs remain visible and collidable at low quality. The bridge's separate rails are replaced by the same continuous curb.

Boundary impacts project incoming velocity onto the wall tangent. Impacts with a normal component above 72% of incoming speed stop the car; glancing contact applies exponential friction once per physics tick, preventing speed-preserving wall riding and extra drag from multiple seam contacts.

- Rules: 39 checks passed.
- `tests/curb_collisions.gd`: 69 checks passed across all three tracks, including both sides, ground and bridge head-on impacts at 49 m/s, glancing friction, containment and clear passage through both crossing levels.
- Visual review: `docs/screenshots/curbs.png`, generated by `tests/capture_curbs.gd`.
- Six-car race regression: player completed three laps in 255.75 s; all six cars had zero resets.
